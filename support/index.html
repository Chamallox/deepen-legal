Deepen Support

Email francois@nomi.so with any question, bug or idea. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong, and you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app.

Common questions

Does it work with paper books?

Yes, and that is the point. Deepen never asks what you are reading on. You log the page you reached and answer out loud, so a library copy and an ebook work the same way.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your books, notes and map live on your iPhone.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the next renewal and you keep access until the period you have paid for ends.

I subscribed but the app says I am not Pro.

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases, which asks Apple for your receipt again. If that does not work, email the address above.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then Delete my data. It removes every book, recap, open question, word card, saved passage and recording from the device. Deleting the app removes them too.
