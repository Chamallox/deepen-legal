Deepen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Deepen is a reading app. You log the pages you have read, it asks you one question, you answer out loud, and your answer becomes concepts on a map.

There is no account

No sign-up, no login, no email address, no password. Deepen never asks who you are.

Your books, notes, recordings, word cards and saved passages are stored on your device. There is no server-side copy of your library.

What is collected, and why

Your recordings and your notes. When you record a recap, or ask a question about what you have read, the audio or the text is sent to be processed and the result comes back to your device. Processing involves an AI provider outside the European Union. Nothing you record, write or say is stored on any server, and no identifier is attached to it.

Anonymous usage counts. Which screens are opened and which buttons are tapped, so friction can be found and fixed. These never contain what you read, wrote, said or looked up: no book title, no author, no note text, no concept, no quote, no question, no word. Settings has a "Share usage data" switch that stops these being sent at all.

A bug report or an idea, only if you write one and tap Send. It contains the text you wrote, plus the app and iOS version for a bug report so it can be reproduced. You choose every word.

Book covers, definitions and imports. Searching for a book, looking up a word, or importing a Goodreads shelf queries that public catalogue directly, carrying no Deepen identifier. Deepen deliberately does not load the cover images Goodreads supplies, because loading them would tell their host which books you own.

Deepen shows no ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Who receives it

Deepen uses service providers to run the app: processing, hosting, anonymous usage counting, and subscriptions. Each acts on Deepen's instructions and is bound to protect what it receives, and each gets only what its job needs. Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers or advertisers.

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple. Deepen never sees your payment details.

Rate limiting, and your IP address

Because there is no account, requests are counted against a random identifier created when you install the app, and against your IP address, so no single install can overwhelm the service. These are counters only: no content is stored with them, they expire within the hour, and they are never used to profile or recognise you.

Microphone, camera and photos

The microphone records only after you tap record, and stops when you stop it.

The camera has two uses, both of which save you typing: reading a passage off the page when you save a quote, and scanning the barcode on a book's back cover to add it to your shelf. It runs only while one of those screens is open. No photo or video is taken, kept or sent.

A passage is recognised on your device and stays there. A scanned barcode is looked up as a number in the public book catalogue, the same lookup as searching for the book by title.

Choosing your own cover image for a book uses your photo library, not the camera. iOS hands Deepen the single image you pick and never the rest of your library.

Children

Deepen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Your library is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Settings also has an option that erases everything Deepen has stored, recordings included, without deleting the app.

There is no account and no server-side copy of your library, so there is nothing else to request deletion of. Usage counts, if you left them on, are tied to a random install identifier and nothing else: no name and no email address, because Deepen has never held either.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because Deepen holds no account and keeps no server-side copy of your library, most of these you can exercise yourself by deleting the app or using the erase option in Settings. For anything else, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the date at the top will change and the new version will appear here.
